Privacy Policy for HarborHelm

Effective date: September 10, 2026

HarborHelm is a sailing and boat-handling simulation game developed and published by Skerry Forge.

Information We Collect

The current version of HarborHelm does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information or sensitive user data.

HarborHelm does not require users to create an account and does not collect information such as names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, or personal identifiers.

Game Data

Game progress, preferences, and settings may be stored locally on the user's device. This locally stored information is not transmitted to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

Locally stored game data can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to the device's own Android backup and restore settings.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of HarborHelm does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect personal information.

Third-Party Services

HarborHelm may be distributed through Google Play. Google may process information independently in connection with downloading, installing, updating, or using apps through Google Play. Such processing is governed by Google's own privacy policies and is not controlled by HarborHelm.

You can read Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Sharing

HarborHelm does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties because the application does not collect such information.

Data Retention and Deletion

HarborHelm does not retain personal user data on developer-operated servers. Locally stored application data can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

HarborHelm does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if HarborHelm's features or data-handling practices change. Any updated version will be published at this location with a revised effective date.
